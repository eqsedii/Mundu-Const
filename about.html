About us

Jenga nasi, jenga kwa ubora. Build with us, build with quality.

A builder you can call, trust and see on site.

Mundu Wangai Construction builds family homes, rental apartments and commercial structures, from the first slab to the last coat of paint. Our motto, Jenga nasi, jenga kwa ubora, means "build with us, build with quality", and it guides every site we run.

One team handles masonry, concrete, roofing, plumbing, electrical, tiling, cabro paving and finishing, so you deal with one contractor and one point of contact from start to handover.
